Before you begin

Frequently asked questions

A few details about supported files, feedback, and your results.

Which report types are available?

The checker loads its available report templates from the analysis service. Choose a type in the list above to see its required sections.

Which file formats can I submit?

You can upload a PDF or DOCX file up to 10 MB, or paste text directly. DOCX is recommended when you want formatting checks.

Is the readiness score an official grade?

No. It is an advisory score based on structure, language, and formatting checks. It is not a grade or a guarantee of acceptance.

Does word count affect the score?

No. Word counts are estimates and are shown as guidance only. They are not included in the score.

Is my report saved in an account?

This checker does not provide accounts or saved report history. Your document is sent to the configured analysis service to produce feedback. Avoid submitting sensitive personal information.

Should I rely only on the checker?

No. Automated checks can miss context or flag text incorrectly. Review suggestions yourself and follow the latest instructions from your supervisor and department.
